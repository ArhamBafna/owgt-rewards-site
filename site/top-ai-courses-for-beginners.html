Top AI Courses for Beginners

A curated list of foundational AI and Machine Learning courses from top platforms, ideal for absolute beginners wanting to learn generative AI and ethics.

#Skool#Community#Guide
The following is a comprehensive list of recommended courses on Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Machine Learning (ML), curated from leading technology companies and prestigious academic institutions. This guide is designed to help you find the right course for your skill level and career goals, whether you are a complete beginner or an aspiring professional. The courses are categorized by creator and cover a range of topics from foundational concepts and practical coding to ethical considerations and prompt engineering. --- ## Foundational AI Courses #### Introduction to Generative AI This course is perfect for beginners and offers practical labs. It focuses on generative AI, a technology that allows you to create new content such as text, images, and code. Participants can experiment and learn by doing, which makes the learning process engaging and effective. * **Creator:** Cloud Skills Boost platform * **For whom:** Absolute beginners. * **What you'll learn:** The basics of generating text, images, and code + practical labs. * **Time:** ~1 hour * **🔗 Link:** https://www.cloudskillsboost.google/course_templates/536 #### Responsible AI This course focuses on the ethical aspects of AI, discussing issues such as bias, fairness, and building ethical AI tools. It provides practical tips on how to create AI solutions that respect social values. * **Creator:** Cloud Skills Boost * **For whom:** Anyone who wants to understand the ethics of AI (bias, fairness, safety). * **Time:** ~1 hour * **🔗 Link:** https://www.cloudskillsboost.google/course_templates/554 #### Introduction to AI and Machine Learning An accelerated course for aspiring machine learning engineers that includes coding exercises. It's a great way to gain practical skills in building and deploying machine learning models. * **Creator:** Cloud Skills Boost * **For whom:** Future ML Engineers (requires basic technical knowledge). * **What you'll learn:** The basics of AI/ML, cloud tools. * **🔗 Link:** https://www.cloudskillsboost.google/course_templates/593 --- ## Academic & Comprehensive Courses #### CS50: Introduction to AI with Python One of the most in-depth introductory courses to AI at the university level. It covers a wide range of topics, including search, machine learning, natural language processing, and more. This course is an excellent choice for those who want to gain a thorough understanding of the fundamentals of AI. * **Creator:** Harvard University * **For whom:** Those seeking deep, academic knowledge. * **What you'll learn:** Search algorithms, ML, NLP, neural networks. * **Time:** ~16 hours * **🔗 Link:** https://pll.harvard.edu/course/cs50s-introduction-artificial-intelligence-python #### AI for Beginners A complete curriculum consisting of 12 lessons, 24 labs, and real-world projects. This course offers a comprehensive introduction to AI, allowing participants to gain practical skills by working on real projects. * **Creator:** Microsoft * **For whom:** Systematic learners who enjoy projects. * **What you'll learn:** A full curriculum in 12 lessons: from neurons to NLP + 24 labs. * **🔗 Link:** https://github.com/microsoft/AI-For-Beginners #### Machine Learning with Python Part of a professional AI certificate. This course focuses on supervised and unsupervised learning, as well as real-world use cases. It's a great opportunity to learn how to apply machine learning techniques in practice. * **Creator:** IBM (on Coursera) * **For whom:** Practitioners who want to learn ML (supervised/unsupervised learning). * **Time:** ~16 hours * **🔗 Link:** https://www.coursera.org/learn/machine-learning-with-python #### Prompt Engineering for AI Models Created in collaboration with leading AI researchers, this course teaches you how to create better prompts for real-world applications. It's a unique opportunity to learn how to effectively communicate with large language models and generative AI systems. * **Creator:** DeepLearning.AI (Andrew Ng) * **For whom:** All users of AI text and image generation models. * **What you'll learn:** How to effectively create prompts for specific tasks. * **🔗 Link:** https://www.deeplearning.ai/short-courses/chatgpt-prompt-engineering-for-developers/ #### AI for Everyone A non-technical course that helps you understand how to think about and work with AI. It is an excellent choice for people who want to understand the impact of AI on business and society, without getting into the technical details. * **Creator:** Andrew Ng (Stanford) on Coursera * **For whom:** Non-technical managers, entrepreneurs, the curious. * **What you'll learn:** How to think about AI, implement it in business, and understand its impact. * **Time:** ~16 hours * **🔗 Link:** https://www.coursera.org/learn/ai-for-everyone #### AI Fundamentals Part of a series of AI certificates for beginners, including quizzes and badges. This course offers an introduction to fundamental AI concepts, allowing participants to earn a certificate that confirms their skills. * **Creator:** IBM (on Coursera) * **For whom:** Beginners who want to earn an AI certificate. * **What you'll learn:** Fundamental AI concepts, business applications + quizzes. * **🔗 Link:** https://www.coursera.org/learn/applied-artifical-intelligence-ibm-watson-ai
