Avoid Chat Limits

Learn how to avoid context rot and chat limits by editing messages, batching requests, and starting fresh chats often.

#Skool#Community#Guide
Every message resends your entire chat history. Message one is tiny. Message 30 is massive. That is why limits hit fast and quality tanks (context rot). Fix the weight. Stop wasting tokens. --- ## 1. Edit Instead of Following Up Correcting a mistake with a new message saves the bad reply, your correction, and the new reply into history. Every future message reprocesses all of that junk. * **Action:** Edit your original message directly. The bad exchange disappears completely. --- ## 2. Batch Requests Into One Prompt Three messages equal three full context reloads. One message with three asks equals one reload. * **Template:** > Do all of the following in one response: > 1. A 3-sentence summary > 2. The 5 key points as bullets > 3. A 10-word headline --- ## 3. Start a Fresh Chat Often Long chats burn millions of tokens reading old history. Reset every 15 to 20 messages. * **Handoff Prompt:** > Summarize this chat as a handoff document: what we are working on, what is done, what is open, and key constraints. Make it short so I can paste it into a new chat. --- ## 4. Turn Off Extended Thinking Extended thinking creates massive output tokens before answering. Output tokens cost way more. Keep it off unless you need heavy reasoning. --- ## 5. Convert Files to Markdown PDFs and HTML carry dead weight like fonts, layouts, and images that drain tokens. * **Action:** Convert files to clean Markdown before uploading. It cuts token weight by up to 90% 📉. --- ## 6. The Session Reset Trick Usage windows start at your very first message of the day. * **Action:** Send a dummy message early in the morning. Come back hours later for real work. You get two solid sessions instead of getting blocked midday. --- ## 7. Work Off-Peak Peak hours bring server congestion and faster session burn. Shift heavy workloads to early mornings, late nights, or weekends.
