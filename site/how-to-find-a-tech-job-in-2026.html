How to Find a Tech Job in 2026

A concise blueprint for breaking into tech and landing a software engineering job in a competitive market.

#Career#Software Engineering#Job Search
#### 1. Master LeetCode - Start practicing daily well before the recruiting cycle begins. - If you don't know where to start, focus on the **Blind 75** list on LeetCode. #### 2. Learn a High-Demand Skill - If your resume isn't getting bites, you need new skills. - Dive deep into **AWS** (via AWS Skill Builder) or **Spring Boot**. - Recommended resource: *Amigoscode* on YouTube. #### 3. Build Something Valuable - Classes and degrees are no longer enough. - Build a significant project that takes at least 3-6 weeks to complete to showcase on your resume. #### 4. Network Relentlessly - "It's not what you know, it's who you know." - Attend career fairs and hackathons. - Message your connections directly when applying for jobs; referrals are the easiest way to get an interview.
