How to Sell $5,000/Month AI Websites

A complete workflow for building high-ticket, automated AI websites for local businesses using GoHighLevel and AI tools.

#Web Design#GoHighLevel#Agency#Sales
Stop selling basic $300 websites generated by ChatGPT. To charge high-ticket retainers ($3k-$5k/month), you must sell **systems**, not just sites. This means integrating CRMs, automated follow-ups, and AI booking tools. #### Step 1: Scrape Competitor Formats - Go to the **Facebook Ad Library** and find competitors in your niche (e.g., bathroom remodeling) that are running ads. - If they are running ads, their website converts. - Copy their URL and use **Firecrawl** to scrape the site for its `markdown` (structure) and `branding` (colors). #### Step 2: Generate the Foundation - Find your target client's existing (outdated) website and scrape their logo/branding. - Paste the competitor's markdown + your client's branding into **Claude AI** or the **GoHighLevel AI Studio**. - Prompt: *"Make me a website similar to this structure, optimized for cold traffic conversions, using this new branding."* #### Step 3: Create Authentic UGC Images with AI - Don't use obvious AI-generated images or stock photos. - Go to **Pinterest**, search for organic UGC (User Generated Content) style photos in the niche. - Use tools like **Hixfield**, **Nano Banana Pro**, or **Midjourney** as an image-to-image generator. - Prompt: *"Generate an image of a modern bathroom, UGC iPhone style picture, using the layout of the reference photo."* - You can even generate perfect "Before & After" split-screen photos using AI. #### Step 4: Add Backend Automation (The $5k Value) - Build the site inside **GoHighLevel** so it natively connects to a CRM. - **Automated Follow-ups**: When a lead fills a form, immediately trigger an SMS ("Hey, got your request, calling in 5 mins!"). - **Reactivation Campaigns**: If a lead goes cold, automate an email/SMS 60 days later to revive them. - **Booking & Payments**: Allow customers to book calendar slots and pay directly on the site. #### Step 5: Sales - Cold call, DM, or SMS local businesses. - Send them a preview link of the completed AI website (built in 10 minutes) before they even pay. - Upsell them on the backend automation and ad-management.
