Title: How To Find A Tech Job In 60 Seconds
Link: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vmN2xwOYvmE

I had four internships at 20, broke into big tech at 21, and now I'm the founder of my own tech startup at 22. If you're struggling to get a job in this job market, this is everything you're going to do this summer to lock in and fix it. First is LeetCode. The 2027 recruiting cycle already started, and if you're not doing one or two LeetCode questions a day, you will not be ready when interviews start coming around. If you don't know where to start, go to LeetCode and do their Blind 75. Next, you need to learn a new skill because clearly your resume wasn't strong enough to be getting into these jobs in the first place. I highly recommend familiarizing yourself with AWS by going to their Skill Builder site or diving deep into Spring Boot because that's what got me my job at Amazon, and I highly recommend Amigoscode on YouTube for that. Next, to continue leveling up your resume, you need to build something of value. Something that would take you at a minimum 3 weeks to 6 weeks to build because your classes and your degree alone are not enough to get a job. Finally, talk to people. It's not what you know, it's who you know. Network, go to career fairs, hackathons, etc. And then when you're looking for jobs, start messaging these connections because this is the easiest way to get your foot into the door. Do all of this, lock in this summer, and I promise you will get hired.
