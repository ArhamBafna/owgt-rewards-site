11. Get Devil's Advocate Feedback
"I have decided to [decision]. Play devil's advocate. Give me the
strongest possible argument against this decision, not a balanced
view, just the case against. Then tell me what I should have in
place before I commit."
12. Analyze Second-Order Consequences
"If [decision or event], what are the second and third-order
consequences for my business? Go one level deeper than the obvious.
Flag the consequence I'm least likely to have considered."
9. Compare Two Real Options 
"I need to decide between [Option A] and [Option B]. For each:
best case, most likely case, worst case. Which will I regret more
in 12 months if it goes wrong?"
10. Build a Decision Framework
"I regularly face decisions about [type of decision]. Build a
simple decision framework I can reuse, no more than five criteria,
with a brief explanation of how to weight each one."
13. Identify the Root Cause 
"Here's a recurring problem: [describe]. Run a five-whys analysis
to find the root cause. Is this structural, skills, or
prioritization?"
14. Make the Decision You've Been Avoiding
"I've been avoiding a decision about [topic]. What I know: [facts].
What I'm uncertain about: [uncertainties]. What decision would you
make, and what are you assuming?"
