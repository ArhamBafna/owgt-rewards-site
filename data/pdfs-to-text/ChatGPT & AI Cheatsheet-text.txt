PROMPTING TECHNIQUES
Zero-shot
Just ask. No examples. Use for simple, well-known tasks.
One-shot
Give one example before the ask. Sets tone fast.
Few-shot
3–5 examples. Best for matching a specific style or pattern.
Chain-of-Thought
Add "think step by step." Forces reasoning before the answer.
Tree-of-Thought
"Brainstorm 5 approaches, evaluate each, then pick the best."
Role-play
"You are X." Locks tone, vocabulary, and POV.
Chained prompts
Output of prompt 1 becomes input to prompt 2. Compounds
quality.
Instructional
Explicit steps. "First do A, then B, then return C."
PRIMING: ZERO → ONE → FEW-SHOT
ZERO
 Write me 15 CTAs about [topic].
ONE
 Write me 15 CTAs about [topic]. Example: "Claim your bonus — code PROMPTGOLD."
FEW
 Write me 15 CTAs about [topic]. Here are 5 examples in the style I want: [ex 1] [ex 2] [ex 3] [ex 4] [ex 5].

CLONE YOUR VOICE
1. Paste 2–3 samples of your writing.
2. Ask: "Analyze the tone and style. Output a bullet-
pointed style guide."
3. Save that style guide as a reusable system prompt.
4. Use it: "Rewrite [text] using the style guide above."

PRO TIPS MOST PEOPLE MISS
Ask for output as CSV or JSON — paste straight into
Sheets / code.
Ingredients > recipes. Feed it your raw data and let it
cook.
Make it argue with itself: "Now critique that answer
harshly."
Always fact-check with a second engine (Perplexity,
Claude, Gemini).
"Ask me 5 questions before answering" → dramatically
better output.
Save your best prompts as reusable templates.
Specify length: "Reply in under 100 words."
For long tasks, ask for a plan first, then approve before
execution.
AI GLOSSARY FOR NON-TECH PEOPLE
Prompt
 The instructions you give the AI.
Input
 Text, image, or file you provide.
Output
 What the AI gives back.
Token
 A chunk of text (~4 chars). Models bill by token.
Context
 Everything the model can "see" right now.
LLM
 Large Language Model (e.g. GPT-4, Claude,
Gemini).
Training Teaching the model patterns from huge datasets.
Fine-tune Custom-train a model on your own examples.
Generative AI that creates new content (text, image, video).
Temperature Creativity dial. Low = safe, high = wild.
Hallucination When the AI invents facts confidently.
Agent
 An AI that uses tools and takes multi-step actions.
RAG
 Retrieval-Augmented Generation — pulls in real
data.
System prompt The hidden master instructions for the AI.
