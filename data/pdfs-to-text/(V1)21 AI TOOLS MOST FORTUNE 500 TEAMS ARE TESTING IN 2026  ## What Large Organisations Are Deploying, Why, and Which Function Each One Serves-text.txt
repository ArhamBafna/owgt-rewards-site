The most underinvested enterprise AI category, and one of the highest ROI. The
problem it solves: institutional knowledge is scattered across Slack, Confluence,
Google Drive, Jira, Salesforce, and email. Employees spend significant time
searching for information that already exists somewhere in the organisation.
ENTERPRISE KNOWLEDGE AND SEARCH
MICROSOFT 365 COPILOT
What it does: AI embedded across the Microsoft 365 suite, Outlook, Teams,
Word, Excel, SharePoint, with access to the Microsoft Graph data layer.
Why enterprises choose it: 90% of Fortune 500 companies already use
Microsoft Cloud services. Copilot integrates AI into the tools employees
already live in, with no new interface to learn and permission-correct access
to the Microsoft stack. For Microsoft-first organisations, it is the lowest-
friction enterprise AI deployment available.
Best for: Large enterprises standardised on Microsoft 365 that want AI
across all productivity tools without a new platform.
MICROSOFT
GLEAN 
What it does: Enterprise search across 100+ workplace applications, Slack,
Google Drive, Confluence, Jira, Salesforce, GitHub, and more, with AI-
powered answers that cite sources and respect access controls.
Why enterprises choose it: Glean is the market leader for enterprise
knowledge retrieval. It solves the problem Microsoft Copilot does not
address outside the M365 ecosystem: finding information across a
heterogeneous tool stack while honoring the permission model for each one.
Best for: Organisations using many different tools (beyond Microsoft) where
employees regularly cannot find institutional knowledge.

Every meeting produces two outputs: what was said, and what happens next.

MEETING INTELLIGENCE
GONG 
What it does: Revenue intelligence platform with meeting recording,
transcript analysis, deal scoring, and sales coaching built in.
Why enterprises choose it: Gong is the market leader for sales conversation
intelligence. It turns every customer call into searchable, analyzable data,
surfacing deal risks, coaching opportunities, and pipeline signals that would
otherwise stay in a rep's head.
Best for: Sales organisations that need to analyse conversations at scale,
coach reps, and forecast pipeline with precision.

FIREFLIES.AI

Teams, and Webex, with 6,000+ integrations for distributing meeting data to

Why enterprises choose it: Fireflies is the most integration-rich meeting tool
available. It captures meetings across any platform and feeds the data into
CRM, project management, and workflow tools automatically. It is particularly
strong for Salesforce and HubSpot workflows. Transcription accuracy runs
90 to 93%.
Best for: Teams that need universal platform coverage and want meeting
data feeding directly into existing systems.

CATEGORY 5
AI CODING AND DEVELOPMENT
GITHUB COPILOT 
What it does: AI code completion, generation, and review integrated directly
into the developer's IDE, VS Code, JetBrains, Vim, and others.
Why enterprises choose it: GitHub Copilot is the most widely deployed AI
coding tool in enterprise environments. It writes code suggestions as
developers type, generates functions from comments, explains existing
code, and reviews pull requests. It integrates into the existing development
workflow without changing how engineers work.
Best for: Engineering teams wanting AI code assistance that integrates into
existing IDE environments without workflow disruption.
GITHUB / MICROSOFT
CURSOR 
What it does: An AI-first code editor built around multi-file code
understanding, with the ability to reference entire codebases when
generating or editing code.
Why enterprises choose it: Cursor operates at a higher level than Copilot.
Where Copilot completes lines and functions, Cursor can understand the
architecture of a full codebase and make changes across multiple files
simultaneously. Developers working on complex systems report it as
qualitatively different from autocomplete-based tools.
Best for: Engineering teams working on complex, multi-file codebases where
codebase-wide understanding produces better output than line-level
completion.

GAMMA 
What it does: AI-generated presentations, documents, and web pages from
a prompt or document input, with design built in.
Why enterprises choose it: Gamma eliminates the blank slide problem. A
prompt produces a structured, designed presentation in seconds. Teams use
it for first drafts, internal presentations, and client-facing decks.
Best for: Any team producing high volumes of presentations who wants to
remove the formatting and design overhead from the process.
GAMMA 
NOTION AI

inside the Notion workspace, for teams already using Notion for
documentation.
Why enterprises choose it: For Notion-centric teams, Notion AI requires no
new platform, no new login, and no new interface. It lives where the
documents already live. It can summarize pages, answer questions about
 a knowledge base, draft new content, and reorganise existing documents.
Best for: Teams using Notion as their primary documentation platform who
want AI without leaving their workspace.

CATEGORY 7
RESEARCH AND INTELLIGENCE
PERPLEXITY 
What it does: AI-powered search that retrieves and synthesizes information
from the web with citations for every claim.
Why enterprises choose it: Perplexity does what generic AI models cannot:
it searches the current web, retrieves live information, and cites its sources.
For research tasks, competitive intelligence, regulatory monitoring, and
market scanning, the citation requirement matters as much as the answer. It
is the right tool when you need to know where the information came from.
Best for: Research, competitive intelligence, legal, and compliance functions
where sourced, verifiable information is required.
PERPLEXITY AI
GROK 
What it does: AI model with real-time access to X (formerly Twitter) data,
surfacing breaking news, market signals, and live conversation before they
reach traditional media.
Why enterprises choose it: Grok is the only mainstream AI model connected
to real-time social data at scale. For functions that depend on being ahead of
breaking developments, investor relations, communications, competitive
intelligence, brand monitoring, Grok surfaces signals that other models
cannot access. News breaks on X before it reaches newsletters.
Best for: Communications, competitive intelligence, and executive teams
that need real-time market signal rather than indexed information.
