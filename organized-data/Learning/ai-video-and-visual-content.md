### Content

What it is: Using Al tools to generate, edit, or enhance visual and video
content, reducing production time without reducing output quality.
Who is hiring: Marketing, creative, and social media teams.

visual content for presentations or social media.
Advanced: Manages an Al content production pipeline combining text,
visual, and video tools at scale.
Tool to start: Gemini for image generation. Gamma for Al presentations.
