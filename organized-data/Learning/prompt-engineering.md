What it is: Designing structured instructions that produce useful, specific
Al outputs instead of just typing questions and hoping.
Who is hiring: Marketing, HR, operations, and product roles. Average
Beginner: Can write a prompt that includes role, context, and format
instructions. Gets consistently better outputs than generic questions.
Advanced: Designs prompt libraries and templates for team use. Evaluates
best practices.
Tool to start: The Al Report's Executive Prompt Library. Study the
structure of each prompt, role, context, task, format, before writing
your own.
