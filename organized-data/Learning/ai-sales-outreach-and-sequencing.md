### Name
AI SALES OUTREACH AND SEQUENCING

### Description
Using Al to personalise outreach at scale, researching prospects, drafting personalised emails and messages, and building sequenced follow-up workflows.

### Content

What it is: Using Al to personalise outreach at scale, researching
prospects, drafting personalised emails and messages, and building
sequenced follow-up workflows.
Who is hiring: Sales development and revenue operations at
B2B companies.
Beginner: Uses Al to research a prospect and draft a personalised
outreach email. Edits and sends.
Advanced: Builds Al-powered outreach sequences with Al handling each
stage, not just the first draft.
Tool to start: Perplexity for prospect research. Claude for message
drafting. The Executive Prompt Library for structured outreach prompts.
