### Name
Evaluate A New Market Opportunity

### Description
A ready-to-use prompt that instructs the AI to: Evaluate this market opportunity: [describe].

### Prompt
```text
Evaluate this market opportunity: [describe]. Assess it across
five dimensions: market size and growth trajectory, competitive
intensity, our right to win given current capabilities, capital
required to compete, and strategic fit with our current direction.
Give me a go/no-go recommendation with the key condition that
would change your view.
```
