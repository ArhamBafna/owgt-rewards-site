### Name
Challenge An Assumption

### Description
A ready-to-use prompt that instructs the AI to: I am operating under the assumption that [state assumption].

### Prompt
```text
I am operating under the assumption that [state assumption].
Steelman this assumption, give me the strongest argument it is
correct. Then tell me the single piece of evidence that would most
quickly prove it wrong.
```
