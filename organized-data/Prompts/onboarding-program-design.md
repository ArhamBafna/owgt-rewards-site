### Name
Onboarding Program Design

### Description
A ready-to-use prompt that instructs the AI to: Design a 30-60-90 day onboarding program for [role].

### Prompt
```text
Design a 30-60-90 day onboarding program for [role]. The
program should ensure that: by day 30 the new hire understands
the business, their role, and key relationships; by day 60
they are contributing independently; by day 90 they have clear
goals and are operating at full pace. For each phase: the
three to five most important activities, the key relationships
to establish, and how success is defined.
```
