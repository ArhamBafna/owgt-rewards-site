### Name
Employee Engagement Analysis

### Description
I am going to share results from an employee engagement survey
or feedback data.

### Prompt
```text
I am going to share results from an employee engagement survey
or feedback data. Help me identify: the two or three themes
that represent our biggest engagement risks, which findings
are most likely driven by structural or systemic issues versus
individual manager quality, the segment of employees most at
risk, and the two actions with the highest potential impact.
Data: [paste in survey results or theme summaries]
```
