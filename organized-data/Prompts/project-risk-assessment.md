### Name
Project Risk Assessment

### Description
A ready-to-use prompt that instructs the AI to: I am about to kick off [project name and description].

### Prompt
```text
I am about to kick off [project name and description]. Before
we start, help me build a project risk assessment. Identify
the ten most likely risks across the categories of scope,
resources, timeline, stakeholder alignment, and external
dependencies. For each risk: probability, potential impact,
and one specific mitigation action. Flag the top three risks
that could derail the project entirely.
```
