### Name
Worst-Case Scenario Planning

### Description
A strategic template to worst-case scenario planning.

### Content
```
I am considering [decision]. Walk me through the worst-case scenario in detail, not the unlikely catastrophic one, but the realistic bad outcome I should be prepared for. Tell me: how bad is it really? Is it recoverable? And what would I need to have in place to recover from it?
```
