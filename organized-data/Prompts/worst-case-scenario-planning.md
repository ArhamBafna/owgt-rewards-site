### Name
Worst-Case Scenario Planning

### Description
I am considering [decision].

### Prompt
```text
I am considering [decision]. Walk me through the worst-case
scenario in detail, not the unlikely catastrophic one, but the
realistic bad outcome I should be prepared for. Tell me: how bad is
it really? Is it recoverable? And what would I need to have in place
to recover from it?
```
