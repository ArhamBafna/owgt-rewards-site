### Name
Meeting Effectiveness Audit

### Description
A ready-to-use prompt that instructs the AI to: I am going to describe our current meeting culture and
structure.

### Prompt
```text
I am going to describe our current meeting culture and
structure. Identify: which meetings are likely providing less
value than their time cost, what meeting hygiene improvements
would have the most impact on team productivity, and what a
well-designed meeting architecture would look like for a team
of [our size and function]. Current state: [describe your
recurring meetings, their cadence, and the general state of
your meeting culture]
```
