### Name
Draft A Product Announcement

### Description
A ready-to-use prompt that instructs the AI to: We are launching [feature or product].

### Prompt
```text
We are launching [feature or product]. The customer problem it
solves is [describe]. Write a product announcement for [email / in-
app / LinkedIn]. Lead with the customer problem, not the feature
name. Under 150 words.
```
