### Name
Decide on Organizational Structure

### Description
A strategic template to decide on organizational structure.

### Content
```
I'm considering reorganizing [describe current structure] to [describe proposed structure]. Evaluate the proposal on: what it optimizes for, what it sacrifices, who is likely to resist it and why, and what would need to be true for it to work. Tell me the one thing I'm most likely underestimating about this change.
```
