### Name
Team Effectiveness Diagnosis

### Description
My team is facing the following challenges: [describe the
symptoms].

### Prompt
```text
My team is facing the following challenges: [describe the
symptoms]. Help me diagnose the root cause by identifying:
whether this looks more like a structural, interpersonal, or
skills issue, the two or three questions I should ask to
confirm my diagnosis, and the intervention that would have the
highest impact if my diagnosis is correct. Team context:
[describe size, structure, and how long these challenges have
been present]
```
