### Name
Design a Weekly Review Framework

### Description
A strategic template to design a weekly review framework.

### Content
```
Design a weekly review framework for a founder at a [stage] company. It should cover: what to review from last week, what to set for next week, and what to let go of. Cap the whole thing at 30 minutes and tell me what to skip.
```
