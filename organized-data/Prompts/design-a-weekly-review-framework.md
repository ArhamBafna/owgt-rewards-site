### Name
Design A Weekly Review Framework

### Description
Design a weekly review framework for a founder at a [stage]
company.

### Prompt
```text
Design a weekly review framework for a founder at a [stage]
company. It should cover: what to review from last week, what to
set for next week, and what to let go of. Cap the whole thing at 30
minutes and tell me what to skip.
```
