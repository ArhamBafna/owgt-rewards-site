### Name
Kpi Framework Design

### Description
I need to design a KPI framework for [team/function].

### Prompt
```text
I need to design a KPI framework for [team/function]. The
framework should cover: two or three leading indicators that
predict future performance, two or three lagging indicators
that confirm results, and one or two health metrics that
prevent us from chasing top-line performance at the cost of
team wellbeing. For each KPI: how it is measured, the data
source, the reporting cadence, and what a good versus
concerning result looks like.
```
