Hire vs. Outsource

### Description

### Prompt
```text
I need [capability] for [purpose]. Evaluate three options: hiring
someone internally, outsourcing to an agency or contractor, or using
AI to partially or fully address the need. For each option, give me
the realistic cost, the timeline to full capability, and the main
risk.
```
