### Name
Apply Devil's Advocate

### Description
A strategic template to apply devil's advocate.

### Content
```
My team has converged on the following decision: [describe]. Play devil's advocate as rigorously as possible. Make the strongest case against this decision, not a balanced view, the strongest possible critique. Don't soften it. Then tell me what evidence would be most likely to convince you the decision is actually right.
```
