### Name
Craft an Ask for a Warm Introduction

### Description
A strategic template to craft an ask for a warm introduction.

### Content
```
I want [person] to introduce me to [target]. Write a short message asking for the intro that: explains why I'm reaching out to them specifically, makes it easy to say yes or no, and includes a two- sentence version of what I do that they can forward.
```
