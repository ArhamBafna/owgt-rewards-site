### Name
Craft An Ask For A Warm Introduction

### Description
I want [person] to introduce me to [target].

### Prompt
```text
I want [person] to introduce me to [target]. Write a short message
asking for the intro that: explains why I'm reaching out to them
specifically, makes it easy to say yes or no, and includes a two-
sentence version of what I do that they can forward.
```
