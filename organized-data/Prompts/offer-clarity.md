### Name
Offer Clarity

### Description
A ready-to-use prompt that instructs the AI to: Help me improve this offer: [describe offer].

### Prompt
```text
Help me improve this offer: [describe offer]. Who is it for, what problem does it solve, what outcome does it create, and what makes it different? Give me 3 better ways to position it.
```
