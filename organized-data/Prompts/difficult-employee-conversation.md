### Name
Difficult Employee Conversation

### Description
A ready-to-use prompt that instructs the AI to: Prep
I need to have a difficult conversation with [an employee,
described by role and situation] abo...

### Prompt
```text
Prep
I need to have a difficult conversation with [an employee,
described by role and situation] about [the issue]. Help me
prepare by: scripting my opening statement so it gets directly
to the point without preamble, anticipating the three most
likely responses and preparing my reply to each, identifying
the specific outcome I need from this conversation, and
planning how to close the conversation regardless of how the
employee responds.
```
