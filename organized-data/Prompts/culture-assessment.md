### Name
Culture Assessment

### Description
A ready-to-use prompt that instructs the AI to: Help me assess the current state of our organisational
culture.

### Prompt
```text
Help me assess the current state of our organisational
culture. Based on the following context and data, identify:
the two or three strongest cultural attributes that define how
we operate at our best, the two or three cultural gaps between
our stated values and daily reality, the specific behaviors
reinforcing the gaps, and two concrete actions leadership
could take to close the most important gap. Context: [describe
your stated values, recent engagement data, and your
observations]
```
