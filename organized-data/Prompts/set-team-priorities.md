### Name
Set Team Priorities

### Description
A strategic template to set team priorities.

### Content
```
My team of [describe] has the following competing demands: [list]. Our most important outcome in the next [timeframe] is [describe]. Build a priority stack that clearly names what we're doing first, what we're doing second, and what we're explicitly not doing. Include one sentence of rationale for each that I can share with the team." "I need to communicate [difficult news] to [audience]. The news is: [describe]. Help me structure the communication so it: leads with the most important fact, explains the reasoning honestly, acknowledges the impact on the audience, and ends with what happens next. Give me the one thing I'm most tempted to soften that I should instead say directly.
```
