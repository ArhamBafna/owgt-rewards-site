### Name
Data Story Narrative

### Description
A ready-to-use prompt that instructs the AI to: I have the following data to present to [describe your
audience]: [paste in or describe your data].

### Prompt
```text
I have the following data to present to [describe your
audience]: [paste in or describe your data]. Help me turn this
into a narrative that: opens with the single most important
finding, builds the supporting evidence in a logical sequence,
explains what the data means for strategy or decisions, and
closes with a clear recommendation. The audience cares most
about: [describe]
```
