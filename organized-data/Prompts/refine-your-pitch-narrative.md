### Name
Refine Your Pitch Narrative

### Description
A powerful prompt to refine your pitch narrative.

### Prompt
```text
Here is my current pitch narrative: [describe or paste]. Identify
the moment in the narrative where an investor's attention is most
likely to drift, and rewrite that section so the logic flows more
compellingly from the problem to our solution.
```
