### Name
Build Your 90-Day Roadmap

### Description
A powerful prompt to build your 90-day roadmap.

### Prompt
```text
I'm the founder of [company at stage X]. Our 90-day goal is
[goal]. Build a week-by-week roadmap with the three highest-
leverage activities per week and the two biggest risks.
```
