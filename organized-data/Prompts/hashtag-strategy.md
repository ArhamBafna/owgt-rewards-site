### Name
Hashtag Strategy

### Description
A ready-to-use prompt that instructs the AI to: Give me 15 relevant hashtags for a post about [topic] targeting [audience] on Instagram.

### Prompt
```text
Give me 15 relevant hashtags for a post about [topic] targeting [audience] on Instagram. Mix of large (1M+), medium (100K-1M), and niche (under 100K).
```
