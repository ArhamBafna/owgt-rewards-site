### Name
Pressure-Test Your Business Idea

### Description
Act as a skeptical investor who has seen thousands of pitches to find the three biggest reasons a business fails.

### Content
"Act as a skeptical investor who has seen thousands of pitches.
Here is my business idea: [describe]. What are the three biggest
reasons this fails, and what specific evidence would change your
mind on each?"
