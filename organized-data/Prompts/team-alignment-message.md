### Name
Team Alignment Message

### Description
A ready-to-use prompt that instructs the AI to: My team is showing signs of misalignment around [describe the
issue].

### Prompt
```text
My team is showing signs of misalignment around [describe the
issue]. Help me draft talking points for a team conversation
that: names the misalignment directly without blame,
reconnects the team to our shared goals and values,
establishes clear expectations going forward, and opens the
door for honest conversation about what is getting in the way.
```
