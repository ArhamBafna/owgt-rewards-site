### Name
Analyze Second-Order Consequences

### Description
A strategic template to analyze second-order consequences.

### Content
```
If [decision], what happens next? Map the first-order consequences. Then, for the two most significant first-order consequences, map the second-order consequences, what happens as a result of those? Flag the second-order consequence I'm least likely to have considered.
```
