### Name
All-Hands Talking Points

### Description
A ready-to-use prompt that instructs the AI to: I am preparing for an all-hands meeting.

### Prompt
```text
I am preparing for an all-hands meeting. The main topic is
[describe the topic]. Draft talking points that: open with the
headline message directly, explain the context and reasoning
in plain language, anticipate the three questions employees
are most likely to have and address them proactively, and
close with a clear call to action or next step.
TEAM COMMUNICATION
```
