### Name
Draft a Weekly Update to Your Team

### Description
A powerful prompt to draft a weekly update to your team.

### Prompt
```text
Draft a weekly update for my team at a [stage] company. This
week: wins were [list], challenges were [list], next week's
priorities are [list]. Write it in a format that takes under two
minutes to read and leaves people clear on what matters.
```
