### Name
Investment Decision Analysis

### Description
A strategic template to investment decision analysis.

### Content
```
I'm deciding whether to [enter/exit] [market or segment]. Here is what I know about it: [describe]. Here is our current position: [describe]. Evaluate the decision on: the strength of our right to win, the opportunity cost of the resources required, and the reversibility of the decision. Tell me the decision I'll regret more in three years: entering too late, or exiting too early.
```
