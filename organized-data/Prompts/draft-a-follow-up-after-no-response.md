### Name
Draft a Follow-Up After No Response

### Description
A powerful prompt to draft a follow-up after no response.

### Prompt
```text
I sent [describe the original outreach] to [person/role] and have
not heard back after [timeframe]. Write a follow-up that: takes a
completely different angle than the first message, is under 60
words, and gives them an easy out if they're not interested.
```
