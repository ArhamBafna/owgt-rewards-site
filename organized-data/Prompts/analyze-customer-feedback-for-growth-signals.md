### Name
Analyze Customer Feedback for Growth Signals

### Description
A powerful prompt to analyze customer feedback for growth signals.

### Prompt
```text
Here is customer feedback I've collected: [paste]. Analyze it for
growth signals, not just the satisfaction data, but what it reveals
about: the customer segments most likely to expand, the use cases
we're underdeveloped in, and the competitor we're most often
replacing or being compared to. Give me the one growth hypothesis
this data most supports.
```
