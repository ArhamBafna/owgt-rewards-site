### Name
Analyze Customer Feedback for Growth Signals

### Description
A strategic template to analyze customer feedback for growth signals.

### Content
```
Here is customer feedback I've collected: [paste]. Analyze it for growth signals, not just the satisfaction data, but what it reveals about: the customer segments most likely to expand, the use cases we're underdeveloped in, and the competitor we're most often replacing or being compared to. Give me the one growth hypothesis this data most supports." "I'm considering investing [describe resource] in [describe opportunity]. Evaluate the investment on: expected return and time to see it, key assumptions that must hold for the return to materialize, what the minimum viable version of this investment looks like, and at what point I should cut my losses if it's not working.
```
