### Name
Create a Meeting Agenda

### Description
A powerful prompt to create a meeting agenda.

### Prompt
```text
I have a [type of meeting] coming up with [attendees]. The one
decision that must come out of this meeting is [describe]. Build a
45-minute agenda that ends with that decision made, and tell me
what I need to prepare in advance.
```
