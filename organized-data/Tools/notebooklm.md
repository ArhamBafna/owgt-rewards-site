### Name
NotebookLM

### Description
AI research assistant that allows you to upload your own documents and ask questions across all of them simultaneously.

### Content
Workflow it replaces: Internal research across multiple documents.
Business outcome: Internal research that required cross-referencing multiple files becomes a single conversation.
Best for: Research teams, consultants, and analysts working across large document libraries.
