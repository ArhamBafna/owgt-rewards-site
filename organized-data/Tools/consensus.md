### Description

### Content

**What it does:** AI research assistant
that allows you to upload your own
documents and ask questions across
all of them simultaneously.
Workflow it replaces: Internal
research across multiple documents.
Instead of searching through filing
systems and reading multiple
sources, NotebookLM treats all your
uploaded documents as a single
searchable knowledge base.
Business outcome: Internal research
that required cross-referencing
multiple files becomes a single
conversation.
Best for: Research teams,
consultants, and analysts working
across large document libraries.
