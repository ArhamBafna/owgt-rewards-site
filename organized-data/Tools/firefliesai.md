### Description

Why enterprises choose it: Fireflies is the most integration-rich meeting tool available. It captures meetings across any platform and feeds the data into CRM, project management, and workflow tools automatically. It is particularly strong for Salesforce and HubSpot workflows. Transcription accuracy runs 90 to 93%.
