### Name
Fireflies.Ai

### Description
Meeting transcription and AI notes across Zoom, Google Meet, Teams, and Webex, with 6,000+ integrations for distributing meeting data to downstream systems.

### Content

**What it does:** Meeting
transcription and AI notes
across Zoom, Google Meet,
Teams, and Webex, with
6,000+ integrations for
distributing meeting data to
downstream systems.
Workflow it replaces: Manual
meeting note-taking and
action item capture. Instead of
someone writing notes during
a call, Fireflies records,
transcribes, and extracts
action items automatically.
Business outcome: Meeting
follow-ups that previously
required manual write-ups are
produced automatically and
synced to CRM and project
tools.
Best for: Teams that need
universal platform coverage
and want meeting data
feeding directly into existing
systems.
