### Name
Paradox

### Description
AI video interview platform that conducts, scores, and ranks candidate video responses, providing structured assessments before human review.

### Content

**What it does:** AI video interview
platform that conducts, scores, and
ranks candidate video responses,
providing structured assessments
before human review.
Workflow it replaces: First-round
video screening by HR teams.
Candidates complete structured video
interviews on their own schedule;
HireVue scores and surfaces top
performers for human review.
Business outcome: Screening capacity
increases significantly without adding
recruiter hours. Interview scheduling
coordination is eliminated.
Best for: Large enterprises with
structured hiring processes and high
applicant volumes.
