### Description
AI writing, summarisation, and Q&A capabilities embedded inside the Notion workspace, for teams already using Notion for documentation. Why enterprises choose it: For Notion-centric teams, Notion AI requires no new platform, no new login, and no new interface. It lives where the documents already live. It can summarize pages, answer questions about a knowledge base, draft new content, and reorganise existing documents.
