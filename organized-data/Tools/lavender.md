### Name
Lavender

### Description
AI email coaching and personalisation tool that scores outbound emails and suggests improvements in real time.

### Content

**What it does:** AI email coaching and
personalisation tool that scores
outbound emails and suggests
improvements in real time.
Workflow it replaces: The manual
editing and review pass on outbound
sales emails. Instead of managers
reviewing rep emails or reps second-
guessing their own writing, Lavender
scores and improves each email
before it sends.
Business outcome: Reply rates
improve. Email quality becomes
consistent across the team rather
than varying by rep.
Best for: Sales teams sending high
volumes of personalised outbound
email.
