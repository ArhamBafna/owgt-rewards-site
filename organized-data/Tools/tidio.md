**What it does:** AI-powered
conversational marketing and sales
qualification through chat, surfacing
intent signals and routing qualified
buyers to sales.
Workflow it replaces: Manual
website lead qualification. Instead of
marketing teams nurturing every
inbound lead manually, Drift qualifies
intent in real time and routes the right
conversations to sales.
Business outcome: Sales teams
spend time on qualified pipeline rather
than first-contact qualification.
Best for: B2B companies with high
inbound traffic and a sales-led growth
motion.
