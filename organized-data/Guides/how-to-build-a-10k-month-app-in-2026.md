### Name
How to Build a $10k/Month App in 2026

### Description
A 60-second blueprint on building and launching a profitable app, from idea generation to deployment and marketing.

### Content

#### 1. Idea Generation
- Stop obsessing over a completely unique idea.
- Use **Sensor Tower** or **Trust MRR** to find apps already generating real revenue.
- Find ways to tweak or improve them to create your own version.

#### 2. Choose Your Stack
- **Web App**: Use **Next.js** for seamless front-end and back-end development.
- **Mobile App**: Use **React Native** and **Expo Go** to build for both Apple and Android simultaneously.

#### 3. Leverage AI Tools
- Familiarize yourself with AI coding assistants.
- **Claude Coder**: Best for front-end design and user experience.
- **Codex**: Best for complex backend architecture.

#### 4. Backend & Payments
- **Database / Backend**: Supabase or Firebase.
- **Payments**: Stripe (Web) or RevenueCat (Mobile).

#### 5. Launch & Distribute
- Release early to get user feedback immediately.
- **Deployment**: Vercel (free for web), App Store ($99/yr), Play Store ($25/yr).
- **Marketing**: Grow organically through TikTok, Instagram, UGC, Reddit, or Product Hunt.
- **Scale**: Once organic works, turn the winning formulas into paid ads.
