### Name
5 AI Businesses to Make $1M (With Zero Employees)

### Description
A breakdown of 5 high-leverage AI service businesses you can start today, including a 4-step framework for launching.

### Content

#### The AI Startup Ladder (4-Step Process)
1. **Validate**: Pick a niche (e.g., med spas, agencies) and do outreach to prove demand.
2. **Pre-sell**: Sell the service and collect cash before building. Use a simple document with a Stripe checkout link.
3. **Deliver**: Once paid, drop everything to manually deliver the result within 48 hours. This builds your reputation and earns referrals.
4. **Build & Automate**: Productize the service to scale deployment.

#### 1. AI Inbox & Calendar Manager (Highest Potential)
- **What it is**: Building an AI executive assistant to manage emails and meetings for busy CEOs.
- **Why it works**: Entrepreneurs desperately want to buy back their time.
- **Income Potential**: $3,000 - $5,000 / day.

#### 2. AI Data Cleanup Agency
- **What it is**: Organizing a company's messy internal data (emails, CRMs, spreadsheets), vectorizing it, and putting an AI search layer on top.
- **Why it works**: Businesses need to make better decisions based on their own data.
- **Income Potential**: $1,500 - $3,000 / day.

#### 3. AI Sales Chatbot
- **What it is**: Creating a chatbot that converts social media chats, texts, and emails into leads and sales.
- **Why it works**: Business owners get massive inbound engagement but lack time to reply.
- **Income Potential**: $1,000 - $1,500 / day.

#### 4. AI Content Repurposing Service
- **What it is**: Turning long-form content (podcasts, webinars) into short-form clips for TikTok/Reels using AI tools.
- **Why it works**: Algorithms favor niche short-form content.
- **Income Potential**: $750 - $1,000 / day.

#### 5. AI Appointment Setter
- **What it is**: Replacing human inbound phone agents with AI voice agents to book sales calls.
- **Why it works**: Cheaper than a human, available 24/7, and performs incredibly well.
- **Income Potential**: $500 - $1,000 / day.
